**Characteristics of a Pragmatic Programmer**

* *Early adopter/fast adapter:* Have an instinct for technologies and techniques. Love trying things out. When given something new, can grasp quickly and integrate it with the already available knowledge.
* *Inquisitive:* Tend to ask questions a lot. Pack rat for little facts, each of which may affect some decisions years from now.
* *Critical thinker:* Rarely take things as given, without first getting the facts.
* *Realistic:* Get a good feel for how difficult things are and how long it will take to solve. This realisation gives the stamina to keep at it.
* *Jack of all trades:* Try hard to be familiar with a broad range of technologies and environments. Work to keep abreast of new developments. Although the current job requires to be a specialist, there's ability to move on to new areas and challenges.
